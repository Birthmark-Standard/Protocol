# relay hops at 180 s

Build `relay_180s`. Record-to-device linking with 60 decoy transactions in flight; end-to-end delay D = 1036.6 s. Per-decision attack; intervals are 95% and resample whole runs. Device level (primary): the named device is the record's. Submission level: the named submission group contains a packet of the record's capture. Rows are real records only. The first hops and the credential processor are scored on every record.
